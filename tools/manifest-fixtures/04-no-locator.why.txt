PROVENANCE ENTRY WITH NO LOCATOR — `document_id` and `revision` name a
document but say nothing about WHERE in it.

figdown-manifest.md §4.5 (`PUBLICATION-MANIFEST-PROFILE`, verifier assertion D) requires every
`provenance` entry to carry `relation` AND at least one locator field —
`section`, `figure`, `locator` or `bbox`. `document_id`/`revision`/`uri`/
`document_sha256` say WHICH document; they are not locators inside it. A
provenance entry with none of the four locator fields names a source and
points at nothing in it, which is not a usable claim under this profile.
carries no locator field
