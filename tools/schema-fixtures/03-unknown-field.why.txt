UNKNOWN FIELD — a Node carrying `colour`.

The model is CLOSED, exactly as the wire grammar is (§8): an unrecognized key
is an error and never something a consumer may ignore. This control is the
whole reason the schema sets `additionalProperties: false` everywhere — a new
engine field must FAIL this gate until the schema and §12.2 are edited to admit
it. `color=` in particular was retired language-wide (`COLOUR-KEY-STATUS`), so a
model carrying it is a model from a language that no longer exists.
